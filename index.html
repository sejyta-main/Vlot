De methode

Every sentence is trained as four separate skills — listening, dictation, writing and speaking — each on its own spaced-repetition clock. The app always serves whatever is weakest, and it makes you produce Dutch, never just recognize it. Speaking climbs a ladder: shadow with text → shadow by ear → say it from the English alone.
